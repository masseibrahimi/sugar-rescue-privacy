Sugar Rescue Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Sugar Rescue ("the game") is a free, ad-supported match-3 game made by Masee. This policy explains what information is used when you play.

Your game progress stays on your phone

Your levels, stars, coins, boosters, home repairs and settings are saved only on your device. The game has no accounts and no servers of ours, and we cannot see this data. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information from your device, such as the advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google uses this information as described in its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later. On any Android phone you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads).

Some ads are optional "bonus" videos that you choose to watch for a reward. Ads never appear in the middle of a move.

Purchases

The current version of the game has no real-money purchases. Coins and boosters are earned by playing or by watching optional ads.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, location or any other personal information, and the game does not use analytics or tracking of its own.

Children

The game is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the game to delete everything it saved. To control ad data, use the ad settings on your phone described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
